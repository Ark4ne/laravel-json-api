---
title: Guards
description: A change of meaning has no shape to declare; read the revision of the request instead.
---

When a value keeps its shape but changes its meaning, there is nothing to declare: read the revision of the rendering.

```php
'sessions' => $this->many(
    LessonSessionResource::class,
    fn() => ApiRevision::current()->isAtMost(ApiRevision::Labels)
        ? $this->resource->sessions
        : $this->resource->sessions->where('user_id', $request->user()?->id),
),
```

`ApiRevision::current()` is the revision of the request, anywhere: a resource, `with()`, `additional()`, a controller.

| Method | True when the current revision is |
| --- | --- |
| `isAtMost(X)` | `X` or older |
| `isAtLeast(X)` | `X` or newer |
| `isBefore(X)` | older than `X` |
| `isAfter(X)` | newer than `X` |

`ApiRevision::using($revision, fn() => …)` renders at another revision.

::warning
A guard is for a change of **meaning**. A change of **shape** (a name, a type, a field that appears) is declared on
the field with [`was()`, `since()` or `until()`](/laravel-json-api/revisions/changing-a-field), so that the definitions,
the validation and the documentation of each revision know it.
::
