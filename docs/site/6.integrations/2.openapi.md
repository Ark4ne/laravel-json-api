title: OpenAPI
description: Generate OpenAPI documents of your JSON:API endpoints from the definitions of your resources.
---

Every resource has a public definition (`PostResource::definition()`): its type, the type of each value, its
relationships and their related resources, links and meta. Tools read it instead of guessing.

:lib-card{slug="laravel-openapi-generator"}

With [Laravel OpenAPI Generator](/laravel-openapi-generator) installed, your resources are documented as JSON:API
resource objects in `application/vnd.api+json`:

- the descriptors give the types and constraints of the attributes (`$this->string()`, `$this->date()`,
  `$this->struct()`…), with their `describe()`, `example()` and `deprecated()`;
- the `Includes` and `Fields` rules of your form requests give the `include` and `fields[type]` parameters;
- the relationships give the `included` resources and their links;
- with `JsonApi::renderErrors()`, the error responses are JSON:API error documents.

With [API revisions](/laravel-json-api/revisions/overview), each revision of the API is its own OpenAPI document, read
at that revision.

```bash [Terminal]
composer require ark4ne/laravel-openapi-generator
php artisan openapi:generate
```

See its [JSON:API integration](/laravel-openapi-generator) for the settings.
