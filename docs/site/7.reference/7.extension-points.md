| Interface | Registered by | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Ark4ne\ApiRevisions\Schema\Descriptor` (extend it) | `TypeRegistry::register('money', MoneySchema::class)` | A [value type](/laravel-json-api/resources/attributes#value-types-of-your-application) of the application: `normalize()`, `type()` |
| `Ark4ne\JsonApi\Http\Errors\ErrorRenderer` | `errors.renderers`, or binding `ErrorRenderers` | [Rendering an exception](/laravel-json-api/requests/errors#error-renderers-of-your-own): `PRIORITY`, `supports()`, `render()` |
| `Ark4ne\JsonApi\Rendering\Stage` | `rendering.stages` | A step of the rendering of every resource object |
| `Ark4ne\JsonApi\Configuration\TypeNaming` | `type.naming` | The type of a resource without `toType()` |

## Reading the definitions

`PostResource::definition()` returns the compiled `Ark4ne\JsonApi\Definition\ResourceDefinition`: type, attributes
and meta as the shapes of `ark4ne/laravel-api-revisions`, relationships with their related classes, named links and
routes. It is the public contract read by tools such as the
[OpenAPI generator](/laravel-json-api/integrations/openapi). At a revision:
`Definitions::for(PostResource::class, $revision)`.
